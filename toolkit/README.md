# Toolkit templates

Written templates a small business can adopt in an afternoon. The [web app](../app/) fills them in with the business's own details (Policies and Breach plan pages). `{{placeholders}}` are replaced by the app, and blanks (`____`) are for the business to complete.

| Item | File | Toolkit controls |
|---|---|---|
| 3. Policy pack | [policies/](policies/): acceptable use; passwords and MFA; access control; devices, updates and backups; personal data protection; clear desk and physical security | GV-3, PR-1 to PR-14, DE-1, DE-4 |
| 4. PDPA checklist (27 checks) | [pdpa-checklist.md](pdpa-checklist.md) (generated from `data/pdpa-checklist.json`) | GV-1, GV-2, GV-5, ID-2, PR-*, RS-* |
| 5. Incident and breach plan | [incident-breach-plan.md](incident-breach-plan.md) | RS-1, RS-2, RS-3, DE-4, RC-1, RC-2 |

The breach register itself (required by the JPDP Guideline on Data Breach Notification, para 14) is a page in the app.

All wording is original. Legal duties were checked against the PDPA text (Act 709), the 2024 amendment commencement order, and the JPDP guidelines and circulars listed in [docs/pdpa-summary.md](../docs/pdpa-summary.md). Not legal advice.

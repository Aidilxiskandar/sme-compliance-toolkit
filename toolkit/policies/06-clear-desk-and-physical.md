# Clear desk and physical security policy

**Business:** {{business}} · **Responsible person:** {{responsible}} · **Effective:** {{date}} · **Review by:** {{review}}

- **Clear desk:** papers with personal data (forms, IC copies, packing lists, files) are put away when not in use and locked up at the end of the day.
- **Clear screen:** lock the screen when you leave it. Screens showing personal data face away from customers and visitors.
- **Equipment:** the server, NAS, CCTV recorder and router are kept in a locked room or cabinet.
- **Printing:** collect printouts straight away. Shred unwanted paper with personal data.
- **Visitors and vendors:** don't leave them alone with business devices or files. A vendor's remote access is turned on only when needed and off afterwards.
- **CCTV:** signs at the entrance; recordings are viewed only by {{responsible}} or people they name, and kept for no more than ______ days unless needed for an incident.

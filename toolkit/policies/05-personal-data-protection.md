# Personal data protection policy

**Business:** {{business}} · **Responsible person / DPO:** {{responsible}} · **Effective:** {{date}} · **Review by:** {{review}}

{{business}} follows the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (as amended in 2024). This policy says how we handle the personal data of customers, staff and anyone else we deal with. It is a starting point, not legal advice.

## What we collect and why
We keep a list of every set of personal data we hold, with why we collect it, where it is, who can see it and how long we keep it (the personal data inventory). We only collect what we need for that purpose.

## Telling people and getting consent
- We give a written privacy notice, in **Malay and English**, when we collect personal data (Notice and Choice Principle).
- We get **explicit consent** before collecting sensitive data (health, religious beliefs, offences, biometrics, political opinions).
- For children under 18, consent comes from a **parent or guardian**.
- We only send marketing messages to people who agreed, and we stop when anyone asks. Requests to stop direct marketing get a written reply within **21 days** (section 43).
- People can withdraw consent in writing, and we then stop processing that data (section 38).
- If we use CCTV, signs at the entrance say so.

## Keeping it safe
We follow the passwords and MFA, access control, and devices, updates and backups policies. We don't share personal data through personal chat apps or "anyone with the link" shares.

## Requests from people
If someone asks for a copy of their data, or asks us to correct it, we reply in writing within **21 days** (sections 31 and 35). We record every request.

## Suppliers and freelancers
Anyone who processes personal data for us (IT vendor, freelancer, payroll, cloud service) has written terms to protect it, to **tell us promptly about any breach**, and to return or delete the data when the work ends.

## Breaches
Any suspected breach is reported to {{responsible}} straight away and handled using the incident and breach plan. Some breaches must be reported to the Commissioner within **72 hours** and to the affected people within **7 days**, and every breach goes in the breach register for at least **2 years**.

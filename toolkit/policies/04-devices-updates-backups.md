# Devices, updates and backups policy

**Business:** {{business}} · **Responsible person:** {{responsible}} · **Effective:** {{date}} · **Review by:** {{review}}

## Devices
- Every business device is listed in the inventory with who uses it.
- Screen lock after 5 minutes or less, firewall on, anti-malware on and updating.
- Laptops and phones have disk encryption turned on.
- Old devices are wiped (factory reset with encryption on) before they are sold, given away or thrown out.

## Updates
- Automatic updates are on for operating systems, apps, browsers, routers and NAS drives.
- Anything that no longer gets security updates is replaced. Until then it is kept off the internet as far as possible.

## Network
- Customer or guest Wi-Fi is separate from the network used by business devices (POS, PCs, CCTV, NAS).
- No ports are opened on the router for the NAS, CCTV or remote desktop. Remote access uses a method with MFA.

# Passwords and MFA policy

**Business:** {{business}} · **Responsible person:** {{responsible}} · **Effective:** {{date}} · **Review by:** {{review}}

## Rules
1. **One login per person.** Everyone has their own account on every system that allows it. Sharing a login hides who did what.
2. **MFA everywhere it's offered**, starting with email, banking, cloud storage, social media and ad accounts, marketplaces, remote access and every admin account. Use an authenticator app or passkey where possible, rather than SMS.
3. **Long, unique passwords.** At least 12 characters. A short sentence works well. Never reuse a business password anywhere else.
4. **Use the business password manager:** ____________________. No passwords on sticky notes, cards, spreadsheets or in browser autosave on shared computers.
5. **Change every default password** on routers, CCTV recorders, NAS drives, printers and POS admin before or as soon as they're installed.
6. **Business accounts belong to the business.** Register them to a business email, not a personal one, with at least two people able to recover them.
7. **Change a password straight away** if you think someone else knows it, and tell {{responsible}}.

## Where a login can't be individual
Some platforms only allow one login. Keep it in the password manager, give access only to the people who need it, turn on MFA, and change the password whenever one of those people leaves.

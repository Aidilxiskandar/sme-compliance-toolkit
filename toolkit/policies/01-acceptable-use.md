# Acceptable use policy

**Business:** {{business}} · **Responsible person:** {{responsible}} · **Effective:** {{date}} · **Review by:** {{review}}

This policy covers everyone who uses {{business}}'s devices, accounts or data: staff, part-timers, interns and freelancers.

## Business devices and accounts
- Use business devices and accounts for business. Light personal use is fine if it doesn't put the business at risk.
- Don't install software or apps on business devices unless the responsible person agrees.
- Don't turn off antivirus, updates, screen locks or other security settings.
- Lock your screen when you step away. Never leave a logged-in device unattended where customers can reach it.

## Your own phone or laptop
- You may use your own device for business only if it has a screen lock, current updates and (for laptops) disk encryption.
- Don't keep customer, patient, student or client data on a personal device unless the responsible person agrees, and delete it when the work is done.
- If a device with business data or logins is lost or stolen, report it straight away.

## Email, messages and the internet
- Be suspicious of any message that is urgent, asks you to log in, asks for payment, or asks to change bank details. Check by phone using a number you already know.
- Don't open attachments or links you weren't expecting.
- Don't send IC copies, health information, bank details or other personal data through personal chat apps or personal email.

## Social media
- Only people the responsible person names may post for the business.
- Don't post photos of customers, patients, children or staff without their (or a parent's) permission.

## Reporting
Tell {{responsible}} **immediately** if you notice anything odd: strange pop-ups, locked files, password reset messages you didn't ask for, customers reporting scam messages in our name, or data sent to the wrong person. Reporting quickly is always the right thing to do, even if you made the mistake.

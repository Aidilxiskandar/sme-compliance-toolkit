# Access control policy

**Business:** {{business}} · **Responsible person:** {{responsible}} · **Effective:** {{date}} · **Review by:** {{review}}

## Need to know
- People only get access to the data and systems their job needs. Interns, part-timers and freelancers get the least.
- Admin rights are kept to the minimum number of people, and admins use a separate admin account only when needed.
- Sensitive data (health records, children's details, IC copies, bank and salary details) is in folders or systems only the people who need it can open.

## Joiners
When someone starts, {{responsible}} gives them their own accounts, from the list in the inventory, and the policies to read and sign.

## Leavers (on their last day)
- [ ] Disable or remove every account they had, including shared ones (change those passwords)
- [ ] Collect business devices, keys and access cards
- [ ] Remove them from WhatsApp/chat groups and shared folders
- [ ] Remove freelancers and former vendors from platforms and remote-access tools when the work ends

## Review
Every 3 months, {{responsible}} checks the account list for every important system and removes anyone who no longer needs access.

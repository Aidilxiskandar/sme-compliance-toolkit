# 05 Pusat Tuisyen Cemerlang (fictional)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Tuition centre (primary and secondary, in person and online) |
| Location | Seremban, Negeri Sembilan (one centre) |
| Staff | 8 (owner/principal, 1 admin, 6 part-time tutors, mostly university students) |
| Size | Small enterprise |
| Owner | Cikgu Kavitha, 41, former school teacher. Organised on paper, informal with tech. |
| Security budget | About RM200 a month |

## How the business runs
- Registration through an **online form** (a free form tool) that asks for the child's name, birth certificate or MyKid number, school, photo, medical notes, and the parents' IC numbers and phone numbers.
- Answers land in a spreadsheet in the owner's **personal free email account**.
- Online classes on a free video-call service; tutors use their own accounts and laptops.
- One WhatsApp group per class with parents, plus tutors messaging students directly.
- Fees paid by bank transfer; parents send receipts on WhatsApp.
- The centre's social media pages post **class photos, results and "top scorer" posters** with children's names and faces.
- 1 office PC and a printer; attendance on paper, entered into the spreadsheet weekly.

## Personal data held
- About 250 current and 600 former students, **almost all minors**: names, birth certificate/MyKid numbers, photos, schools, exam results, medical/allergy notes.
- Parents' names, IC numbers, phone numbers, addresses.
- Tutors' IC and bank details.

## What the owner says
- "Parents are happy for us to post results, it's good for the kids."
- "Only the admin and I can see the registration sheet."
- "The tutors are students, they're good with tech."

## What is actually true (planted gaps)
1. The registration form asks for far more than needed: parents' IC numbers and medical notes aren't required to run a class.
2. The registration spreadsheet is shared with **all tutors as editors**, including tutors who left, and one link was shared publicly by mistake.
3. Photos and results of minors are posted without written consent, and a parent's request to take down a photo was ignored for weeks.
4. Video-call links are reused every week and posted in public-facing groups, so anyone with the link can join a children's class.
5. Tutors message students directly on personal phones, with no rules or supervision.
6. The owner's personal email has no 2-step verification and is used for everything, including the business's social media logins.
7. Former students' data is never deleted. Records go back 8 years.
8. The office PC is shared, with no password, and the browser saves every password.

## Incident history
- A parent complained that someone outside the class joined an online lesson and posted inappropriate messages in the chat before being removed.
